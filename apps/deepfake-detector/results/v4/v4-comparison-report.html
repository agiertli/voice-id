Deepfake Voice Detector: V4 Experiment Report

Class-weight ablation on expanded dataset (97 clips, 3 speakers) — 24 September 2026
Trained on Apple M3 MPS • Wav2Vec2-XLSR 315M params • Heavy freeze (top 12 transformer layers)

Final Recommendation

Deploy V4-A as the new gary-finetuned.

V4 produces dramatically better probability separation than the currently deployed model (median gap 0.97 vs 0.79). It nearly eliminates false positives (FPR 3.3% vs 33.3%) while maintaining strong fake detection. The class-weight ablation confirmed that V3's collapse was caused by aggressive augmentation, not class imbalance — V4-A and V4-B are functionally identical, so the simpler unweighted model is preferred.

One caveat: V4 has a blind spot for XTTS-cloned speech from held-out recordings (5/6 XTTS-jakub clips misclassified). Fish S2 Pro detection is also slightly lower (71% vs 90%). For a conference demo where the main risk is falsely accusing a human of being AI, the 30-point FPR improvement far outweighs these trade-offs. For a production system, the XTTS blind spot would need investigation.

XTTS blind spot: V4 perfectly detects Fish-cloned Jakub speech but treats XTTS-cloned Jakub speech as natural. Five XTTS-jakub clips score p_fake < 0.02 — indistinguishable from real speech to the model. This suggests V4 learned XTTS artifacts that are specific to the training speakers and don't generalize. The deployed model catches all XTTS-jakub clips but only because it over-predicts FAKE (FPR 33%).

The good news: The natural jakub clip scores 0.002 despite the model having seen 10 Jveverka recordings. The model is learning synthetic vs. natural characteristics, not speaker identity.

Class weights had zero measurable effect. V4-A and V4-B are functionally identical across every metric. This confirms two things:
1. The V3 collapse was not caused by class imbalance (1.7:1 fake:real ratio).
2. It was caused by aggressive augmentation (noise injection + lowpass filtering) destroying the discriminative signal.

V4-A is preferred as the simpler model — no class weighting needed.

Known Limitations & Next Steps

IssueSeverityMitigation
XTTS blind spot on unseen speaker recordings Medium Add XTTS clips from more diverse speakers. The model may have memorized XTTS artifacts specific to Anton/Miska timbres.
Fish S2 Pro detection dropped to 71% Medium The 6 missed Fish clips may benefit from more Fish training data. Mean p_fake for Fish is 0.66 (above threshold but borderline).
Silence standardization breaks both V4 and deployed model Low Both models use silence patterns as a feature. For the game, clips are served as-is, so this doesn't affect gameplay.
Only 3 real speakers — no true speaker-OOD evaluation possible Low Collect recordings from additional speakers. The same-speaker holdout confirms the model isn't memorizing identity.
Small validation set (14 clips) makes per-epoch metrics noisy Low Full-dataset evaluation (this report) is the reliable measure. Val metrics only guide checkpoint selection.
