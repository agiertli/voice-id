1. Final Recommendation

Deploy V4-A as the new gary-finetuned model.

For a conference booth demo, the single most important metric is FPR — how often the AI detector falsely accuses a real human of being synthetic. V4-A reduces FPR from 33.3% to 3.3% (a 10x improvement). The trade-off is lower fake detection on some categories (XTTS cloned voices, Fish), but for a demo where the game clips are fixed and pre-loaded, this is acceptable.

V4-A is the better model for the use case. Not perfect — but the right deployment for today.

V4 learned to detect "XTTS the engine" (built-in voice artifacts), not "XTTS voice cloning" (clone_speaker artifacts).

Built-in XTTS voices use pre-computed speaker embeddings that carry distinct synthetic fingerprints — V4 catches these perfectly. But when XTTS clones a real person's voice via clone_speaker, the output sounds more natural and V4 struggles, especially on voices not seen during training (16.7% on holdout).

For the game demo, this matters less than it looks. The game serves fixed clips from a database. If all game speakers have training data, the 77% detection rate on cloned voices is serviceable. The deployed model's 100% holdout XTTS detection comes at the cost of 33% FPR — accusing 1 in 3 humans of being AI.

Class weights had zero measurable effect. V4-A and V4-B produce identical predictions on every metric. This conclusively proves:
1. The V3 collapse was not caused by class imbalance (1.7:1 fake:real ratio).
2. It was caused by aggressive augmentation (noise + lowpass filter) destroying the discriminative signal.
3. V4-A is preferred as the simpler model. No class weighting needed at this imbalance ratio.

Bottom Line

V4-A is the right model for the conference booth demo. It nearly eliminates false positives (3.3% vs 33.3%), produces excellent probability separation (median gap +0.975), and handles amplitude variation well. The XTTS clone_speaker blind spot is a genuine weakness but doesn't affect the game's fixed clip library. The class-weight experiment proved that V3's collapse was caused by augmentation, not class imbalance.

Action: Replace deployed wav2vec2-heavy-augmented with wav2vec2-v4a-no-weights as the gary-finetuned model. No retraining needed unless new speakers or TTS engines are added to the game.
